# Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

- Scheme Name: Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
- Objective: Provide short-term credit support to farmers for cultivation, working capital, and related agricultural needs.
- Eligibility: Farmers with landholding records and eligible banking relationships can apply for KCC through participating banks.
- Benefits: Affordable short-term credit, revolving credit facility, and easier access to finance for crop cultivation and allied activities.
- Required Documents: Identity proof, land records, bank account details, and application forms required by the lending bank.
- Application Process: Apply through scheduled commercial banks, regional rural banks, cooperative banks, or other authorized lenders.
- Official Website: https://www.nabard.org/
- Applicable States: All Indian states and Union Territories
- Applicable Crops: All crops
- Farmer Category: Farmers requiring crop credit and working capital
- FAQs: NABARD and participating banks provide guidance and related credit information.
