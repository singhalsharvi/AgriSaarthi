# Pradhan Mantri Annadata Aay Sanrakshan Abhiyan (PM-AASHA)

- Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Annadata Aay Sanrakshan Abhiyan (PM-AASHA)
- Objective: Protect farmers from price volatility by providing price support and market assurance for selected crops.
- Eligibility: Farmers growing notified crops and covered under the applicable procurement and market intervention processes are eligible.
- Benefits: Price support, market intervention, and assurance for selected crops during periods of low market prices.
- Required Documents: Farmer identity proof, land records, crop details, and any required registration documents under the state procurement system.
- Application Process: Register with the relevant procurement or state agriculture department channels and follow scheme-specific procedures for notified crops.
- Official Website: https://pm-aasha.gov.in/
- Applicable States: States and districts where the scheme components are notified
- Applicable Crops: Selected crops notified under PM-AASHA
- Farmer Category: Farmers cultivating notified crops
- FAQs: Official PM-AASHA information and support resources are available on the portal.
