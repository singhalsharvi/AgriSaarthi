# National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture (NMSA)

- Scheme Name: National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture (NMSA)
- Objective: Promote sustainable agriculture through natural resource conservation, water-use efficiency, soil health management, and rainfed-area development.
- Eligibility: Farmers, farmer groups, and implementing agencies in rainfed and resource-constrained regions can participate under the mission components.
- Benefits: Support for sustainable practices, improved water and soil management, resilient farming systems, and resource conservation.
- Required Documents: Farmer identity and land details, project or intervention details, and documents required by the implementing agency.
- Application Process: Apply through the relevant state agriculture department or NMSA implementation channels for the particular component.
- Official Website: https://nmsa.dac.gov.in/
- Applicable States: All Indian states and Union Territories, with special relevance to rainfed areas
- Applicable Crops: All crops, especially rainfed and resource-constrained crops
- Farmer Category: Farmers in rainfed and sustainable agriculture intervention areas
- FAQs: The NMSA portal provides information and linked resources for scheme components.
