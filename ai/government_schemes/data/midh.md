# Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH)

- Scheme Name: Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH)
- Objective: Promote holistic growth of the horticulture sector through area-based strategies, technology adoption, and post-harvest infrastructure support.
- Eligibility: Farmers, horticulture producers, farmer groups, and implementing agencies in eligible states and horticulture clusters can participate.
- Benefits: Support for orchard development, protected cultivation, post-harvest management, processing, marketing, and skill development.
- Required Documents: Identity proof, land/holding records, project details, and application materials required by the state implementation system.
- Application Process: Apply through the respective state horticulture department or the implementing agencies under the MIDH framework.
- Official Website: https://midh.gov.in/
- Applicable States: All Indian states and Union Territories
- Applicable Crops: Fruits, vegetables, flowers, spices, aromatic plants, coconut, cashew, cocoa, bamboo, and related horticulture crops
- Farmer Category: Horticulture farmers, grower groups, and producer organizations
- FAQs: The MIDH portal provides scheme information and related resources.
