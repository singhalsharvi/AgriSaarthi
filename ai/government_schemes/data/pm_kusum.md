# Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan (PM-KUSUM)

- Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan (PM-KUSUM)
- Objective: Promote solar irrigation and renewable energy adoption to support farmers and improve energy access in agriculture.
- Eligibility: Farmers, farmer groups, cooperatives, panchayats, and other eligible entities in participating states may be eligible under the scheme components.
- Benefits: Solar irrigation pumps, grid-connected solar power support, and reduced dependence on diesel or grid-based power.
- Required Documents: Identity proof, land records, project details, and application documents required by the implementing agency.
- Application Process: Apply through the relevant state nodal agency or designated implementation channels under the PM-KUSUM components.
- Official Website: https://pmkusum.mnre.gov.in/
- Applicable States: States and UTs implementing PM-KUSUM components
- Applicable Crops: All crops, especially where irrigation access is relevant
- Farmer Category: Farmers and eligible farmer-based entities
- FAQs: Official PM-KUSUM information and guidelines are available through the scheme portal.
