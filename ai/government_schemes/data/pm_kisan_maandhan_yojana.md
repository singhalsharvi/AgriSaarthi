# Pradhan Mantri Kisan Maandhan Yojana (PM-KMY)

- Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Maandhan Yojana (PM-KMY)
- Objective: Provide old-age pension security to eligible small and marginal farmers and similar vulnerable workers.
- Eligibility: Small and marginal farmers and eligible unorganized workers within the prescribed age and income criteria under the scheme.
- Benefits: Assured monthly pension after reaching the eligible age, with matching government contribution.
- Required Documents: Identity proof, bank account details, age proof, and scheme enrollment documents as required by the platform.
- Application Process: Apply through the official PM-KMY or related labour welfare portal and complete enrollment and contribution formalities.
- Official Website: https://maandhan.in/
- Applicable States: All Indian states and Union Territories
- Applicable Crops: All crops, as the scheme targets farmer livelihood security rather than crop-specific support
- Farmer Category: Small and marginal farmers and eligible unorganized workers
- FAQs: The official portal provides scheme information and help resources.
