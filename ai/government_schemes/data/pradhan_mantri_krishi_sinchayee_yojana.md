# Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY)

- Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY)
- Objective: Expand irrigation coverage and improve water use efficiency through source creation, distribution, and micro-irrigation interventions.
- Eligibility: Farmers, water-user associations, and implementing agencies in eligible areas can participate through state-level implementation processes.
- Benefits: Better irrigation access, improved water productivity, higher crop resilience, and support for micro-irrigation systems such as drip and sprinkler.
- Required Documents: Land records, project or beneficiary details, identity proof, and application forms as required by the implementing agency.
- Application Process: Apply through the relevant state agriculture department or authorized PMKSY portal channels, depending on the component.
- Official Website: https://pmksy.gov.in/
- Applicable States: All Indian states and Union Territories
- Applicable Crops: All crops, especially water-sensitive crops and irrigated agriculture
- Farmer Category: Farmers, farmer groups, and implementing agencies
- FAQs: The PMKSY portal includes FAQ and guideline resources for scheme components.
