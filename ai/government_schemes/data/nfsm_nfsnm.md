# National Food Security Mission / National Food Security and Nutrition Mission (NFSM/NFSNM)

- Scheme Name: National Food Security Mission / National Food Security and Nutrition Mission (NFSM/NFSNM)
- Objective: Increase production and productivity of priority crops to strengthen food security and nutrition security.
- Eligibility: Farmers and implementing agencies in notified districts and states can participate through the mission framework.
- Benefits: Support for seeds, demonstrations, crop management practices, and productivity-enhancing interventions for priority crops.
- Required Documents: Farmer identity and land details, along with documents required by the state or implementing authority.
- Application Process: Participate through the relevant state agriculture department and mission implementation channels for the notified interventions.
- Official Website: https://nfsm.gov.in/
- Applicable States: States and districts covered under the mission
- Applicable Crops: Rice, wheat, pulses, coarse cereals, millets, commercial crops, and related priority crops
- Farmer Category: Farmers in notified districts and crop-specific intervention areas
- FAQs: The NFSM portal provides scheme information and related resources.
