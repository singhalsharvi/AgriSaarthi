# National Mission on Edible Oils – Oil Palm (NMEO-OP)

- Scheme Name: National Mission on Edible Oils – Oil Palm (NMEO-OP)
- Objective: Increase domestic production of edible oil by expanding oil palm cultivation in suitable regions.
- Eligibility: Farmers and growers in eligible districts and states can participate through the mission framework.
- Benefits: Support for oil palm cultivation, planting material, inputs, and cultivation assistance.
- Required Documents: Identity proof, land records, and documents required by the implementing authority for oil palm cultivation support.
- Application Process: Apply through the relevant state agriculture department or oil palm implementation channels.
- Official Website: https://oilpalm.dac.gov.in/
- Applicable States: States and districts where oil palm cultivation is promoted
- Applicable Crops: Oil palm
- Farmer Category: Farmers and growers in eligible oil palm areas
- FAQs: The official NMEO-OP portal provides scheme information and related resources.
