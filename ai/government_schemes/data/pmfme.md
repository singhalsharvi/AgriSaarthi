# Pradhan Mantri Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises (PMFME)

- Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises (PMFME)
- Objective: Support micro food processing enterprises through formalization, credit, technology, and marketing assistance.
- Eligibility: Micro food processing enterprises and related entrepreneurs in eligible categories can participate under the scheme.
- Benefits: Credit-linked subsidy, training, common infrastructure, and support for formalization and value addition.
- Required Documents: Enterprise registration details, identity proof, business documents, and documents required by the implementing agency.
- Application Process: Apply through the designated PMFME implementation channels and participating state agencies.
- Official Website: https://pmfme.mofpi.gov.in/
- Applicable States: All Indian states and Union Territories
- Applicable Crops: Food crops and commodities relevant to micro food processing enterprises
- Farmer Category: Farmers, producer groups, and micro food processing entrepreneurs
- FAQs: The official PMFME portal provides scheme details and help resources.
