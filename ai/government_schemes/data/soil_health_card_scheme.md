# Soil Health Card Scheme

- Scheme Name: Soil Health Card Scheme
- Objective: Promote balanced and site-specific nutrient management through soil testing and scientific fertilizer recommendations.
- Eligibility: Farmers who get their soil tested through authorized labs and state agriculture departments are eligible to receive a Soil Health Card.
- Benefits: Improved fertilizer use, better soil fertility management, reduced input costs, and higher crop productivity.
- Required Documents: Farmer identity details, land details, and soil sample submission records as required by the testing process.
- Application Process: Submit soil samples through authorized soil testing labs or designated state processes; receive the Soil Health Card with recommendations.
- Official Website: https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in/
- Applicable States: All Indian states and Union Territories
- Applicable Crops: All crops
- Farmer Category: Farmers across all categories who participate in soil testing
- FAQs: The official Soil Health Card portal provides guidance, resources, and support information.
