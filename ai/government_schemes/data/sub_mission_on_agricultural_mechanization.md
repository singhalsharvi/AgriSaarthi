# Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM)

- Scheme Name: Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM)
- Objective: Promote farm mechanization to improve productivity, reduce labour dependency, and support efficient farm operations.
- Eligibility: Farmers, farmer groups, and institutions participating through state-level mechanization programs are eligible subject to scheme rules.
- Benefits: Subsidy support for farm machinery, improved access to equipment, and better timeliness of agricultural operations.
- Required Documents: Identity proof, land records, machine purchase details, and application documents required by the state or implementing agency.
- Application Process: Apply through the designated state agriculture department or the official mechanization portal and follow the subsidy procedure.
- Official Website: https://agrimachinery.nic.in/
- Applicable States: All Indian states and Union Territories
- Applicable Crops: All crops
- Farmer Category: Farmers and farmer groups seeking mechanization support
- FAQs: The official digital platform provides FAQs and support links.
