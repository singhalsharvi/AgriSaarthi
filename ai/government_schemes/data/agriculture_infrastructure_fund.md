# Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)

- Scheme Name: Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)
- Objective: Finance post-harvest, logistics, storage, and value-chain infrastructure projects to strengthen agriculture infrastructure.
- Eligibility: Farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations, agri-entrepreneurs, cooperatives, and other eligible entities undertaking qualifying infrastructure projects.
- Benefits: Credit guarantee, interest subvention, and financing support for infrastructure projects such as warehouses, cold chains, and processing units.
- Required Documents: Project details, entity registration documents, KYC, bank account details, and project reports as required by lending institutions.
- Application Process: Register on the AIF portal, submit project details, and coordinate with participating lending institutions for sanction and disbursement.
- Official Website: https://agriinfra.dac.gov.in/
- Applicable States: All Indian states and Union Territories
- Applicable Crops: All crops, with a focus on post-harvest and value-chain infrastructure
- Farmer Category: Farmers, FPOs, cooperatives, startups, and agri-entrepreneurs
- FAQs: The AIF portal includes FAQs and support resources for applicants.
