# National Mission on Natural Farming (NMNF)

- Scheme Name: National Mission on Natural Farming (NMNF)
- Objective: Promote natural farming practices that reduce chemical dependence and support sustainable agriculture.
- Eligibility: Farmers and farmer groups in participating areas can participate through the mission implementation framework.
- Benefits: Training, support for natural farming practices, reduced input costs, and improved sustainability.
- Required Documents: Farmer identity proof, land details, and documents required by the state or implementing agency.
- Application Process: Register through the relevant state or central implementation channels for natural farming programmes.
- Official Website: https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in/National_Mission_on_Natural_Farming
- Applicable States: Participating states and regions implementing natural farming programmes
- Applicable Crops: All crops, with emphasis on chemical-free and sustainable cultivation
- Farmer Category: Farmers interested in natural farming
- FAQs: The official Soil Health Card portal provides information and links related to the mission.
