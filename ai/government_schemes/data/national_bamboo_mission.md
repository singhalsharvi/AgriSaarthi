# National Bamboo Mission (NBM)

- Scheme Name: National Bamboo Mission (NBM)
- Objective: Support cultivation, value addition, and market development for bamboo to improve livelihoods in bamboo-growing areas.
- Eligibility: Farmers, producer groups, entrepreneurs, and institutions engaged in bamboo cultivation or value-chain activities under the mission are eligible subject to scheme guidelines.
- Benefits: Support for bamboo planting, processing, value addition, and market linkage.
- Required Documents: Identity proof, land or activity details, project or enterprise documents, and any required registration papers.
- Application Process: Apply through the relevant state or central implementing agencies as per the current NBM guidelines.
- Official Website: https://www.nbm.nic.in/
- Applicable States: States and districts with bamboo cultivation and related activities
- Applicable Crops: Bamboo
- Farmer Category: Farmers, groups, and entrepreneurs involved in bamboo cultivation and processing
- FAQs: Official NBM resources and scheme information are available through the mission portal.
