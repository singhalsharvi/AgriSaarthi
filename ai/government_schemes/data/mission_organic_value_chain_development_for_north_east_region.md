# Mission Organic Value Chain Development for North East Region (MOVCD-NER)

- Scheme Name: Mission Organic Value Chain Development for North East Region (MOVCD-NER)
- Objective: Promote organic farming, value chain development, and market access in the North Eastern Region.
- Eligibility: Farmers, groups, and enterprises in the North Eastern Region engaged in organic farming or related value-chain activities can participate under the scheme.
- Benefits: Support for organic production, certification, processing, aggregation, and market development.
- Required Documents: Identity proof, land or activity records, group/enterprise registration documents, and other documents required by the implementing authority.
- Application Process: Apply through the designated implementing agencies and state-level channels for the North Eastern Region.
- Official Website: https://movcdner.gov.in/
- Applicable States: North Eastern states and eligible regions
- Applicable Crops: Organic crops and produce relevant to the North Eastern Region
- Farmer Category: Farmers and producer groups in the North East engaged in organic agriculture
- FAQs: The official MOVCD-NER portal provides scheme and support information.
