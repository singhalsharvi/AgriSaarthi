# Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana (RKVY)

- Scheme Name: Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana (RKVY)
- Objective: Support state-level agricultural development through planning, infrastructure creation, and productivity enhancement projects.
- Eligibility: States, Union Territories, and implementing agencies can access support; farmers benefit through state-level projects and interventions.
- Benefits: Project-based support for agriculture infrastructure, extension, seed systems, value chains, and productivity improvement.
- Required Documents: Project proposals, administrative and financial documents, and state-level implementation records as required by the scheme.
- Application Process: States submit proposals and implementation plans through the designated RKVY process and state departments.
- Official Website: https://rkvy.da.gov.in/
- Applicable States: All Indian states and Union Territories
- Applicable Crops: All crops, depending on the state project focus
- Farmer Category: Farmers benefiting through state and district-level interventions
- FAQs: Official RKVY information is available through the portal and related department resources.
