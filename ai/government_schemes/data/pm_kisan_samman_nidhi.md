# Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

- Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)
- Objective: Provide direct income support to eligible landholding farmer families to support cultivation and household needs.
- Eligibility: Small and marginal farmer families meeting the scheme criteria; institutional landholders and certain higher-income or excluded categories are not eligible.
- Benefits: Financial support of ₹6,000 per year in three equal installments.
- Required Documents: Aadhaar-linked bank account details, identity proof, and landholding/eligibility details as verified by the state or UT.
- Application Process: Register through the official PM-KISAN portal or authorized local channels, complete verification, and track status online.
- Official Website: https://pmkisan.gov.in/
- Applicable States: All Indian states and Union Territories
- Applicable Crops: All agricultural crops
- Farmer Category: Small and marginal farmer families
- FAQs: Official FAQs and helpdesk information are available on the PM-KISAN portal.
