# e-NAM (National Agriculture Market)

- Scheme Name: e-NAM (National Agriculture Market)
- Objective: Create a unified national market for agricultural commodities and improve price discovery through digital trading.
- Eligibility: Farmers, traders, commission agents, and market participants registered on the e-NAM platform can participate subject to mandi-level rules.
- Benefits: Better price realization, transparent trading, digital auctioning, and wider market access.
- Required Documents: Farmer identity proof, bank account details, land records or farm records, and registration details as required by the mandi and platform.
- Application Process: Register on the e-NAM portal, connect with the appropriate mandi, and follow the platform registration and trading process.
- Official Website: https://enam.gov.in/web/
- Applicable States: All participating states and mandis
- Applicable Crops: Agricultural commodities listed in e-NAM markets
- Farmer Category: Farmers, traders, and market participants
- FAQs: The e-NAM portal provides resources and support information for users.
