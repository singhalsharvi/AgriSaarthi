# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

- Scheme Name: Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
- Objective: Provide crop insurance coverage to farmers against yield loss, crop damage, and localized risks.
- Eligibility: Farmers who have availed crop loans or are enrolled under the notified crop insurance process; coverage depends on the season and crop notified by the scheme.
- Benefits: Financial protection against crop loss, reduced insurance premium burden, and claim support through the insurance mechanism.
- Required Documents: Aadhaar, bank account details, land records, crop details, and application or policy documents as required by the insurer.
- Application Process: Apply through the official PMFBY portal, bank, or local insurance/department channels during the notified enrollment period.
- Official Website: https://pmfby.gov.in/
- Applicable States: All Indian states and Union Territories
- Applicable Crops: Food grains, oilseeds, horticulture, and other notified crops
- Farmer Category: Loanee and non-loanee farmers covered under notified crop insurance operations
- FAQs: The official PMFBY portal provides FAQ and grievance-support resources.
