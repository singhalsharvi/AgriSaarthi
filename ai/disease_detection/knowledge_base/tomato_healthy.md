# Tomato - Healthy

## Crop
Tomato

## Disease
Healthy

## Description
A profile of Healthy affecting Tomato plants. This condition primarily impacts the none with a severity rating of none.

## Symptoms
Vigorous plant with dark green, fully expanded leaflets. Stems are thick and hairy. Flowers set fruit successfully, and tomatoes ripen uniformly according to variety.

## Visual symptoms
Affected plants display symptoms on none: Vigorous plant with dark green, fully expanded leaflets. Stems are thick and hairy. Flowers set fruit successfully, and tomatoes ripen uniformly according to variety.

## Causes
N/A

## Risk factors
Severity is rated none. Key risk factors include planting in fields with a history of infection, poor sanitation, and not sanitizing farming tools.

## Environmental conditions
6-8 hours of direct sunlight daily, well-draining soil rich in organic matter (pH 6.2-6.8), consistent soil moisture, and staking/caging for support.

## Prevention
Prune suckers to maintain single or double leader stems. Apply organic mulch. Scout weekly for early signs of disease.
Rotate tomato planting site annually. Mulch to prevent soil splash.

## Management/treatment
No treatment required. Continue regular watering and fertilization schedules.
