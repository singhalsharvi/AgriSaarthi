# Pepper bell - Bacterial Spot

## Crop
Pepper bell

## Disease
Bacterial Spot

## Description
A profile of Bacterial Spot affecting Pepper bell plants. This condition primarily impacts the leaves, stems, and fruits. with a severity rating of high. defoliation reduces yield and results in sunscald damage to the pepper fruits..

## Symptoms
Small, water-soaked, yellowish-green spots on leaves, which gradually turn dark brown and develop a greasy appearance. Severely infected leaves turn yellow and drop off, exposing fruit to sunscald.

## Visual symptoms
Affected plants display symptoms on leaves, stems, and fruits.: Small, water-soaked, yellowish-green spots on leaves, which gradually turn dark brown and develop a greasy appearance. Severely infected leaves turn yellow and drop off, exposing fruit to sunscald.

## Causes
Bacterium Xanthomonas campestris pv. vesicatoria. It survives in crop residue, volunteer plants, and seed.

## Risk factors
Severity is rated high. defoliation reduces yield and results in sunscald damage to the pepper fruits.. Key risk factors include planting in fields with a history of infection, poor sanitation, and not sanitizing farming tools.

## Environmental conditions
Warm temperatures (75-85°F), high relative humidity, and frequent rain or overhead sprinkler irrigation.

## Prevention
Use certified disease-free seeds and transplants. Practice a 2-3 year crop rotation away from solanaceous crops. Keep fields free of weeds and crop debris.
Decontaminate tools, machinery, and boots before moving to uninfected areas. Do not work in fields when plants are wet.

## Management/treatment
Apply copper-based bactericides early in the season when symptoms first appear. Avoid overhead watering to reduce wetness duration on leaves.
