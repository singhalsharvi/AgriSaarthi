# Potato - Healthy

## Crop
Potato

## Disease
Healthy

## Description
A profile of Healthy affecting Potato plants. This condition primarily impacts the none with a severity rating of none.

## Symptoms
Green, well-expanded compound leaves. Stems are upright and vigorous. Tubers are firm, smooth, and free of discoloration.

## Visual symptoms
Affected plants display symptoms on none: Green, well-expanded compound leaves. Stems are upright and vigorous. Tubers are firm, smooth, and free of discoloration.

## Causes
N/A

## Risk factors
Severity is rated none. Key risk factors include planting in fields with a history of infection, poor sanitation, and not sanitizing farming tools.

## Environmental conditions
Well-drained, sandy loam soil with pH 5.2-6.0. Regular watering (1-2 inches per week) and cool growing temperatures (60-70°F).

## Prevention
Practice hilling to protect growing tubers from sun and pests. Implement crop rotation and maintain soil organic matter.
Use organic mulching to regulate soil temperature and conserve moisture.

## Management/treatment
No treatment required. Continue regular scouting and cultivation.
