# Tomato - Bacterial Spot

## Crop
Tomato

## Disease
Bacterial Spot

## Description
A profile of Bacterial Spot affecting Tomato plants. This condition primarily impacts the leaves, stems, and fruits. with a severity rating of high. causes significant defoliation, exposing fruit to sunscald and decreasing marketability..

## Symptoms
Small, dark, circular spots (less than 3mm) on leaves, surrounded by a yellow halo. Spots can merge, leading to a scorched appearance and leaf drop. Dark, raised specks appear on green fruit.

## Visual symptoms
Affected plants display symptoms on leaves, stems, and fruits.: Small, dark, circular spots (less than 3mm) on leaves, surrounded by a yellow halo. Spots can merge, leading to a scorched appearance and leaf drop. Dark, raised specks appear on green fruit.

## Causes
Bacterium Xanthomonas campestris pv. vesicatoria. The bacteria can spread via wind-driven rain, contaminated tools, and seeds.

## Risk factors
Severity is rated high. causes significant defoliation, exposing fruit to sunscald and decreasing marketability.. Key risk factors include planting in fields with a history of infection, poor sanitation, and not sanitizing farming tools.

## Environmental conditions
High temperatures (75-85°F) accompanied by wet weather, overhead irrigation, or heavy dew.

## Prevention
Use certified disease-free seeds and disease-resistant tomato varieties. Practice 2-3 year crop rotation away from solanaceous plants.
Do not prune or work in the field when plants are wet. Sanitize pruning tools frequently with a 10% bleach solution.

## Management/treatment
Apply copper-based fungicides/bactericides early. Streptomycin sprays can be used on transplants before field setting.
