# Tomato - Leaf Mold

## Crop
Tomato

## Disease
Leaf Mold

## Description
A profile of Leaf Mold affecting Tomato plants. This condition primarily impacts the leaves and occasionally flowers/fruits. with a severity rating of moderate. leads to leaf loss, which reduces fruit quality and size..

## Symptoms
Pale green or yellow spots on the upper surface of older leaves. A velvety, olive-green to purplish-brown mold growth develops on the corresponding underside of the leaves.

## Visual symptoms
Affected plants display symptoms on leaves and occasionally flowers/fruits.: Pale green or yellow spots on the upper surface of older leaves. A velvety, olive-green to purplish-brown mold growth develops on the corresponding underside of the leaves.

## Causes
Fungus Passalora fulva (formerly Fulvia fulva). Spores survive in greenhouse structures and soil.

## Risk factors
Severity is rated moderate. leads to leaf loss, which reduces fruit quality and size.. Key risk factors include planting in fields with a history of infection, poor sanitation, and not sanitizing farming tools.

## Environmental conditions
High relative humidity (>85%) and moderate temperatures (70-75°F). Most common in greenhouses and poorly ventilated tunnels.

## Prevention
Increase spacing between plants and prune lower leaves. Maintain greenhouse relative humidity below 85% through heating and ventilation.
Sanitize greenhouse structures, stakes, and tools between seasons.

## Management/treatment
Apply preventive copper-based or sulfur fungicides. Prune affected leaves to improve airflow.
