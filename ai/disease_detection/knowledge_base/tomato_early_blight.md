# Tomato - Early Blight

## Crop
Tomato

## Disease
Early Blight

## Description
A profile of Early Blight affecting Tomato plants. This condition primarily impacts the leaves, stems, and fruits. with a severity rating of moderate. causes defoliation from the bottom up, leading to sunscald and reduced yield..

## Symptoms
Circular brown spots with concentric 'target' rings on older lower leaves. Spots enlarge and leaf turns yellow and drops. Stem lesions are dark and slightly sunken.

## Visual symptoms
Affected plants display symptoms on leaves, stems, and fruits.: Circular brown spots with concentric 'target' rings on older lower leaves. Spots enlarge and leaf turns yellow and drops. Stem lesions are dark and slightly sunken.

## Causes
Fungus Alternaria solani. Spores are spread by wind, splashing rain, and flea beetles.

## Risk factors
Severity is rated moderate. causes defoliation from the bottom up, leading to sunscald and reduced yield.. Key risk factors include planting in fields with a history of infection, poor sanitation, and not sanitizing farming tools.

## Environmental conditions
Warm temperatures (75-80°F) and wet conditions (dew, rain, or overhead irrigation). Common in plants with poor nutrition.

## Prevention
Mulch the soil around plants to prevent spores from splashing onto lower leaves. Maintain crop rotation. Provide balanced NPK fertilization.
Staking or caging plants keeps foliage off the ground and improves air circulation, reducing moisture buildup.

## Management/treatment
Prune lower leaves to improve airflow. Apply protectant fungicides like chlorothalonil, mancozeb, or copper-based sprays.
