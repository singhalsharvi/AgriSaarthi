# Tomato - Tomato Mosaic Virus

## Crop
Tomato

## Disease
Tomato Mosaic Virus

## Description
A profile of Tomato Mosaic Virus affecting Tomato plants. This condition primarily impacts the leaves, stems, and fruits. with a severity rating of high. stunts growth and reduces fruit quality and quantity..

## Symptoms
Light and dark green mottled 'mosaic' patterns on leaves. Leaves may be puckered, distorted, or reduced in size (shoe-stringing/fern-leaf). Fruits may show internal brown necrosis.

## Visual symptoms
Affected plants display symptoms on leaves, stems, and fruits.: Light and dark green mottled 'mosaic' patterns on leaves. Leaves may be puckered, distorted, or reduced in size (shoe-stringing/fern-leaf). Fruits may show internal brown necrosis.

## Causes
Tobamovirus (ToMV). Highly stable virus spread mechanically via hands, tools, clothes, and contact between plants. It can survive on seeds.

## Risk factors
Severity is rated high. stunts growth and reduces fruit quality and quantity.. Key risk factors include planting in fields with a history of infection, poor sanitation, and not sanitizing farming tools.

## Environmental conditions
Moderate temperatures and active agricultural activities (pruning, tying, harvesting) that facilitate mechanical transfer.

## Prevention
Use certified virus-free seeds or treat seeds with trisodium phosphate (TSP). Plant resistant varieties. Wash hands with soap and water or milk before handling plants.
Disinfect tools in a 20% dry milk solution or commercial disinfectant (e.g., Virkon S) regularly during pruning.

## Management/treatment
No cure. Promptly remove and burn infected plants. Do not compost infected material.
