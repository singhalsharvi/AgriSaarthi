# Tomato - Target Spot

## Crop
Tomato

## Disease
Target Spot

## Description
A profile of Target Spot affecting Tomato plants. This condition primarily impacts the leaves, stems, and fruits. with a severity rating of moderate. can cause significant defoliation and direct fruit lesions, reducing market value..

## Symptoms
Foliar spots are small, brown, and circular with concentric rings, similar to early blight but often more numerous. On fruits, lesions start as small water-soaked spots, turning slightly sunken and dark brown.

## Visual symptoms
Affected plants display symptoms on leaves, stems, and fruits.: Foliar spots are small, brown, and circular with concentric rings, similar to early blight but often more numerous. On fruits, lesions start as small water-soaked spots, turning slightly sunken and dark brown.

## Causes
Fungus Corynespora cassiicola. It can survive in crop debris and on alternative weed hosts.

## Risk factors
Severity is rated moderate. can cause significant defoliation and direct fruit lesions, reducing market value.. Key risk factors include planting in fields with a history of infection, poor sanitation, and not sanitizing farming tools.

## Environmental conditions
High humidity, free moisture on leaves (dew/rain), and temperatures around 70-85°F.

## Prevention
Promote rapid drying of foliage by staking, pruning, and wider plant spacing. Rotate crops with non-susceptible hosts.
Clean crop residues immediately after harvest. Maintain field sanitation.

## Management/treatment
Apply protectant fungicides (mancozeb, chlorothalonil). Systemic fungicides like strobilurins (e.g., azoxystrobin) can be used.
