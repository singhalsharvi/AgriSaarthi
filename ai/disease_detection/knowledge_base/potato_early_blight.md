# Potato - Early Blight

## Crop
Potato

## Disease
Early Blight

## Description
A profile of Early Blight affecting Potato plants. This condition primarily impacts the leaves, stems, and tubers. with a severity rating of moderate. can cause early defoliation, reducing tuber size and yield..

## Symptoms
Dark brown or black circular spots with concentric rings ('target board' pattern) on older leaves first. Spots may merge, causing leaves to yellow and dry up.

## Visual symptoms
Affected plants display symptoms on leaves, stems, and tubers.: Dark brown or black circular spots with concentric rings ('target board' pattern) on older leaves first. Spots may merge, causing leaves to yellow and dry up.

## Causes
Fungus Alternaria solani. It overwinters in soil and crop debris.

## Risk factors
Severity is rated moderate. can cause early defoliation, reducing tuber size and yield.. Key risk factors include planting in fields with a history of infection, poor sanitation, and not sanitizing farming tools.

## Environmental conditions
Alternating wet and dry periods, warm temperatures (75-80°F), and nutrient-stressed or mature plants.

## Prevention
Ensure adequate nitrogen fertilization to prevent plant stress. Plant certified seed tubers. Clean crop debris at the end of the season.
Practice crop rotation with non-host plants (e.g., corn, grains). Handle tubers carefully at harvest to prevent wounding.

## Management/treatment
Apply protectant fungicides (e.g., chlorothalonil, mancozeb) or copper-based sprays. Avoid overhead irrigation.
