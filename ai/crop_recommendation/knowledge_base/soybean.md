# Soybean (Glycine max)

- **Crop Name**: Soybean (Glycine max)
- **Crop Category**: Oilseed / Legume
- **Suitable Temperature**: 22°C - 32°C
- **Humidity Requirement**: 60% - 80%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 650 mm - 1000 mm
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.0 - 7.5
- **Soil Type**: Deep Black Cotton Soil, Heavy Clay Loam, Vertisols with good organic content
- **NPK Requirements**: Low N (N: 20-30 kg/ha), High P (P: 60-80 kg/ha), Moderate K (K: 40 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 30:60:40 + 20 kg Sulphur/ha. Seed treatment with Bradyrhizobium japonicum.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Kharif (June to October).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Karnataka, Telangana.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Mostly rainfed; critical irrigation at flowering and pod filling stage.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Seed rate 70-75 kg/ha; row spacing 45 cm; weed control with Imazethapyr post-emergence.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Soybean (Glycine max) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (Low N (N: 20-30 kg/ha), High P (P: 60-80 kg/ha), Moderate K (K: 40 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Kharif (June to October).).
