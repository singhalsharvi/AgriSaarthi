# Mung Bean / Green Gram (Vigna radiata)

- **Crop Name**: Mung Bean / Green Gram (Vigna radiata)
- **Crop Category**: Short-duration Pulse Legume
- **Suitable Temperature**: 25°C - 35°C (Warm tropical climate)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 50% - 70%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 600 mm - 900 mm
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.2 - 7.5
- **Soil Type**: Well-drained Loam, Sandy Loam, Alluvial soil.
- **NPK Requirements**: Low Nitrogen (N: 20 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 40-50 kg/ha), Low K (K: 20 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 20:40:20 basal application. Seed treatment with Rhizobium and PSB inoculant.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Kharif, Spring, and Summer (Short duration 60-70 days). Excellent for crop rotation.
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Bihar, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Punjab.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Kharif is mostly rainfed; Summer crop requires 3-4 light irrigations every 10-12 days.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Seed rate 15-20 kg/ha; spacing 30x10 cm; foliar spray of 2% DAP at flowering enhances yield.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Mung Bean / Green Gram (Vigna radiata) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (Low Nitrogen (N: 20 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 40-50 kg/ha), Low K (K: 20 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Kharif, Spring, and Summer (Short duration 60-70 days). Excellent for crop rotation.).
