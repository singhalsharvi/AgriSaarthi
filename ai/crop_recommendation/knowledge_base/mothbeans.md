# Moth Bean / Dew Bean (Vigna aconitifolia)

- **Crop Name**: Moth Bean / Dew Bean (Vigna aconitifolia)
- **Crop Category**: Arid Pulse Crop
- **Suitable Temperature**: 24°C - 35°C (High drought and heat tolerance)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 40% - 60%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 300 mm - 600 mm (Extremely drought-hardy)
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.5 - 8.5 (Tolerant to saline/alkaline soils)
- **Soil Type**: Sandy soils, Sandy Loam, Arid desert soils with good drainage.
- **NPK Requirements**: Low Nitrogen (N: 10-15 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 30-40 kg/ha), Low K (K: 10-20 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: Basal application of 20 kg N and 40 kg P2O5 per ha. Seed treatment with Rhizobium inoculant.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Kharif (July sowing with first monsoonal rain). Maturity in 60-75 days.
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Rajasthan, Gujarat, Haryana, Punjab, Maharashtra.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Strictly rainfed; requires minimal water; provides excellent soil cover preventing wind erosion.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Seed rate 10-15 kg/ha; line spacing 30 cm; minimal weeding required due to dense ground cover.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Moth Bean / Dew Bean (Vigna aconitifolia) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (Low Nitrogen (N: 10-15 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 30-40 kg/ha), Low K (K: 10-20 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Kharif (July sowing with first monsoonal rain). Maturity in 60-75 days.).
