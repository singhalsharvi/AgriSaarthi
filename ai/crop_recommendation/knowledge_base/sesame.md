# Sesame / Til (Sesamum indicum)

- **Crop Name**: Sesame / Til (Sesamum indicum)
- **Crop Category**: Oilseed Crop
- **Suitable Temperature**: 25°C - 35°C
- **Humidity Requirement**: 50% - 70%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 400 mm - 700 mm
- **Soil pH Range**: 5.5 - 7.5
- **Soil Type**: Well-drained Sandy Loam, Light Loam soil
- **NPK Requirements**: Moderate N (N: 40 kg/ha), Low P (P: 25 kg/ha), Low K (K: 20 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 40:25:20. Apply full P & K + half N basal.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Kharif and Summer.
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Gujarat, West Bengal, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Sensitive to waterlogging; 2 light irrigations for summer crop.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Seed rate 4-5 kg/ha mixed with dry sand for uniform broadcast; thinning at 15 DAS.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Sesame / Til (Sesamum indicum) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (Moderate N (N: 40 kg/ha), Low P (P: 25 kg/ha), Low K (K: 20 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Kharif and Summer.).
