# Rice (Oryza sativa)

- **Crop Name**: Rice (Oryza sativa)
- **Crop Category**: Cereal / Staple Grain
- **Suitable Temperature**: 20°C - 35°C (Optimum ~25°C - 30°C)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 80% - 90% (High humidity required)
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 1500 mm - 3000 mm (High water requirement / standing water during early growth)
- **Soil pH Range**: 5.5 - 7.0 (Slightly acidic to neutral)
- **Soil Type**: Clay, Silty-Clay, Clay Loam with poor drainage (water retentive soils)
- **NPK Requirements**: High Nitrogen (N: 80-120 kg/ha), Moderate Phosphorus (P: 40-60 kg/ha), Moderate Potassium (K: 40-60 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: Urea for N split applications, SSP/DAP for P, MOP for Potassium. Biofertilizers: Azospirillum, Blue-green algae.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Kharif (June to November) in North/Central India; round-the-year in South/East coastal regions.
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: West Bengal, Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Odisha, Bihar, Assam, Chhattisgarh, Karnataka.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Submerged / flooded conditions (5 cm standing water) during vegetative phase; alternate wetting and drying during flowering.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Puddling and transplanting 21-25 day old seedlings; SRI (System of Rice Intensification) for water saving; weed management at 20 and 40 DAT.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Rice (Oryza sativa) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (High Nitrogen (N: 80-120 kg/ha), Moderate Phosphorus (P: 40-60 kg/ha), Moderate Potassium (K: 40-60 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Kharif (June to November) in North/Central India; round-the-year in South/East coastal regions.).
