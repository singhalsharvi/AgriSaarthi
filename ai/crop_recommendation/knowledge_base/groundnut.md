# Groundnut / Peanut (Arachis hypogaea)

- **Crop Name**: Groundnut / Peanut (Arachis hypogaea)
- **Crop Category**: Oilseed / Legume
- **Suitable Temperature**: 22°C - 30°C
- **Humidity Requirement**: 50% - 75%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 500 mm - 1000 mm
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.0 - 7.5
- **Soil Type**: Well-drained Sandy Loam, Red Loam, Light Alluvial soil; loose texture required for peg penetration
- **NPK Requirements**: Low N (N: 25 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 50 kg/ha), Moderate K (K: 40 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 25:50:40 + Gypsum 400 kg/ha applied at flowering (supplies Calcium & Sulphur for pod development).
- **Suitable Seasons**: Kharif (June-July) and Summer (Jan-Feb).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Telangana.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Light frequent irrigation; critical stages: Flowering, Pegging, and Pod development.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Seed rate 100-110 kg kernel/ha; earthing up at flowering; seed treatment with Trichoderma.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Groundnut / Peanut (Arachis hypogaea) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (Low N (N: 25 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 50 kg/ha), Moderate K (K: 40 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Kharif (June-July) and Summer (Jan-Feb).).
