# Coconut (Cocos nucifera)

- **Crop Name**: Coconut (Cocos nucifera)
- **Crop Category**: Perennial Plantation / Oilseed Crop
- **Suitable Temperature**: 20°C - 34°C (Tropical coastal climate, non-frost)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 60% - 90%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 1500 mm - 2500 mm well-distributed throughout the year
- **Soil pH Range**: 5.2 - 8.0 (Adapts to sandy coastal soils)
- **Soil Type**: Coastal Sand, Red Loam, Laterite, Alluvial, Riverine Loam.
- **NPK Requirements**: High Potassium (K: 1.2 kg K2O/palm/year), Moderate N (0.5 kg N/palm/year), Moderate P (0.32 kg P2O5/palm/year)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: Apply 500g N, 320g P2O5, 1200g K2O per bearing palm per year in two split applications (May-June & Sept-Oct). Organic FYM 50 kg/palm.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Perennial (Harvesting every 30-45 days year-round).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Odisha, Goa, Andaman & Nicobar Islands.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Basin irrigation or drip irrigation (60-80 liters/palm/day). Drip fertigation improves yield.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Planting in 1m x 1m x 1m pits spaced 7.5m x 7.5m; mulching around palm basins; intercropping with spices/banana.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Coconut (Cocos nucifera) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (High Potassium (K: 1.2 kg K2O/palm/year), Moderate N (0.5 kg N/palm/year), Moderate P (0.32 kg P2O5/palm/year)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Perennial (Harvesting every 30-45 days year-round).).
