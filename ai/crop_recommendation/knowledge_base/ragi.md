# Ragi / Finger Millet (Eleusine coracana)

- **Crop Name**: Ragi / Finger Millet (Eleusine coracana)
- **Crop Category**: Nutri-Cereal / Hardy Millet
- **Suitable Temperature**: 20°C - 34°C
- **Humidity Requirement**: 55% - 80%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 500 mm - 1000 mm
- **Soil pH Range**: 5.5 - 7.5
- **Soil Type**: Red Sandy Loam, Light Black soil, Gravelly soil
- **NPK Requirements**: Moderate N (N: 60 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 30 kg/ha), Moderate K (K: 30 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 60:30:30 + 5 t/ha FYM. Apply 50% N + full P & K at transplanting.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Kharif (June-July sowing).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Uttarakhand, Odisha, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Mostly rainfed; supplementary irrigation during dry spells.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Transplanting 25-day old nursery seedlings or direct line sowing; seed rate 5 kg/ha.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Ragi / Finger Millet (Eleusine coracana) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (Moderate N (N: 60 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 30 kg/ha), Moderate K (K: 30 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Kharif (June-July sowing).).
