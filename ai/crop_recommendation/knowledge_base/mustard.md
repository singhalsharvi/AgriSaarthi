# Mustard / Rapeseed (Brassica juncea)

- **Crop Name**: Mustard / Rapeseed (Brassica juncea)
- **Crop Category**: Rabi Oilseed Crop
- **Suitable Temperature**: 10°C - 25°C (Cool growth, dry harvest)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 45% - 65%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 350 mm - 600 mm
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.0 - 7.5
- **Soil Type**: Loam, Alluvial, Light Sandy Loam with good drainage
- **NPK Requirements**: High N & P (N: 80 kg/ha, P: 40 kg/ha, K: 40 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 80:40:40 + 40 kg Sulphur/ha (essential for oil synthesis). Apply 50% N + full P & K at sowing.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Rabi (October/November sowing; harvested Feb/March).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, Madhya Pradesh, West Bengal, Assam.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: 2 irrigations: First at 30 DAS (flower initiation), second at 60 DAS (pod filling).
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Seed rate 4-5 kg/ha; row spacing 30 cm; monitor for Mustard Aphids.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Mustard / Rapeseed (Brassica juncea) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (High N & P (N: 80 kg/ha, P: 40 kg/ha, K: 40 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Rabi (October/November sowing; harvested Feb/March).).
