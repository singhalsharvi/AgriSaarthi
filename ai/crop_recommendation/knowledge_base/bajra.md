# Bajra / Pearl Millet (Pennisetum glaucum)

- **Crop Name**: Bajra / Pearl Millet (Pennisetum glaucum)
- **Crop Category**: Coarse Cereal / Arid Millet
- **Suitable Temperature**: 25°C - 38°C (High heat and drought tolerance)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 40% - 65%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 350 mm - 600 mm (Extremely drought-hardy)
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.5 - 8.5 (Tolerant to saline/alkaline soils)
- **Soil Type**: Sandy, Desert Sandy Soil, Light Loam with good drainage
- **NPK Requirements**: Moderate N (N: 60-80 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 40 kg/ha), Low-Moderate K (K: 20-30 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 80:40:30. Apply 50% N + full P & K at sowing, 50% N top dressed at 30 DAS.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Kharif (June to October).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Rajasthan, Gujarat, Haryana, Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Mostly rainfed; if dry spell occurs, 1-2 irrigations at flowering and grain filling.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Seed rate 4-5 kg/ha; spacing 45x15 cm; excellent crop for low rainfall arid zones.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Bajra / Pearl Millet (Pennisetum glaucum) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (Moderate N (N: 60-80 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 40 kg/ha), Low-Moderate K (K: 20-30 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Kharif (June to October).).
