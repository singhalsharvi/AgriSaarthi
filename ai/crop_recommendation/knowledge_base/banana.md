# Banana (Musa acuminata / balbisiana)

- **Crop Name**: Banana (Musa acuminata / balbisiana)
- **Crop Category**: Tropical Fruit Crop
- **Suitable Temperature**: 15°C - 35°C (Optimum ~26°C - 30°C)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 75% - 90%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 1200 mm - 2200 mm (High water consumer)
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.0 - 7.5 (Neutral fertile soil)
- **Soil Type**: Deep fertile Clay Loam, Alluvial soil with rich organic matter and excellent drainage.
- **NPK Requirements**: Very High Nitrogen & Potassium (N: 200-250g, P: 50-60g, K: 200-300g per plant/year)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 200:50:300g per plant applied in 4-6 split doses at 2nd, 3rd, 4th, and 5th months after planting.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Planted year-round (Main planting June-July or Sept-Oct). Crop cycle 12-14 months.
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Kerala, Bihar, Uttar Pradesh.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Drip irrigation essential (15-25 liters/plant/day). Avoid water stagnation.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Tissue culture plantlets or sword suckers; pit size 60x60x60 cm; spacing 1.8x1.8 m or 1.5x1.5 m; desuckering & propping with bamboo poles.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Banana (Musa acuminata / balbisiana) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (Very High Nitrogen & Potassium (N: 200-250g, P: 50-60g, K: 200-300g per plant/year)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Planted year-round (Main planting June-July or Sept-Oct). Crop cycle 12-14 months.).
