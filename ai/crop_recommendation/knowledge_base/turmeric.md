# Turmeric (Curcuma longa)

- **Crop Name**: Turmeric (Curcuma longa)
- **Crop Category**: Spice / Commercial Crop
- **Suitable Temperature**: 20°C - 35°C
- **Humidity Requirement**: 70% - 85%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 1500 mm - 2200 mm
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.0 - 7.5
- **Soil Type**: Rich Sandy Loam, Red Loam, Clay Loam with excellent drainage and rich organic matter
- **NPK Requirements**: High N & K (N: 120 kg/ha, P: 60 kg/ha, K: 120 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 120:60:120 + 25 t/ha FYM. Apply N and K in 3 split doses at 45, 90, and 120 DAS.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Kharif (May to July planting; harvested Feb-March). Duration 8-9 months.
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala, Odisha, Maharashtra.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Frequent light irrigations (15-20 irrigations/crop); avoid standing water to prevent rhizome rot.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Rhizome setts rate 2000-2500 kg/ha; ridge/raised bed system; green leaf mulching at planting.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Turmeric (Curcuma longa) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (High N & K (N: 120 kg/ha, P: 60 kg/ha, K: 120 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Kharif (May to July planting; harvested Feb-March). Duration 8-9 months.).
