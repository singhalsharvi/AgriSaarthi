# Sugarcane (Saccharum officinarum)

- **Crop Name**: Sugarcane (Saccharum officinarum)
- **Crop Category**: Perennial Cash / Commercial Crop
- **Suitable Temperature**: 20°C - 38°C (Hot humid climate for growth, cool dry for ripening)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 60% - 85%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 1500 mm - 2500 mm (High water requirement)
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.5 - 7.5
- **Soil Type**: Deep fertile Alluvial, Heavy Clay Loam, Black Vertisol soil
- **NPK Requirements**: Very High Nitrogen & Potassium (N: 250 kg/ha, P: 75 kg/ha, K: 115 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 250:75:115. Apply N in 3 splits (sowing, 45 DAS, 90 DAS). Organic FYM 25 t/ha.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Eksali (Jan-Feb), Adsali (July-Aug in South India). Crop duration 10-14 months.
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, Bihar.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Heavy irrigation required every 10-12 days (25-30 irrigations/crop cycle); trash mulching retains soil moisture.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: 3-budded setts planted in ridges and furrows; earthing up at 90 and 120 days; detrashing lower leaves.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Sugarcane (Saccharum officinarum) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (Very High Nitrogen & Potassium (N: 250 kg/ha, P: 75 kg/ha, K: 115 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Eksali (Jan-Feb), Adsali (July-Aug in South India). Crop duration 10-14 months.).
