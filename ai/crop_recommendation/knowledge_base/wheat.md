# Wheat (Triticum aestivum)

- **Crop Name**: Wheat (Triticum aestivum)
- **Crop Category**: Rabi Cereal Staple
- **Suitable Temperature**: 10°C - 25°C (Cool growth, warm sunny harvest)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 50% - 70%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 450 mm - 750 mm
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.0 - 7.5
- **Soil Type**: Deep Alluvial, Clay Loam, Silt Loam with good drainage
- **NPK Requirements**: High Nitrogen (N: 120 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 60 kg/ha), Moderate K (K: 40 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 120:60:40. Apply 50% N + full P & K at sowing; remaining N at first irrigation (CRI stage).
- **Suitable Seasons**: Rabi (November sowing; harvested March/April).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Bihar.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: 4-6 irrigations. Crown Root Initiation (CRI) at 21 DAS is most critical.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Seed rate 100 kg/ha; line sowing at 22.5 cm row spacing; weed control with Clodinafop/Sulfosulfuron.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Wheat (Triticum aestivum) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (High Nitrogen (N: 120 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 60 kg/ha), Moderate K (K: 40 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Rabi (November sowing; harvested March/April).).
