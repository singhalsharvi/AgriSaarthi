# Cotton (Gossypium hirsutum / arboreum)

- **Crop Name**: Cotton (Gossypium hirsutum / arboreum)
- **Crop Category**: Commercial Fiber Crop
- **Suitable Temperature**: 21°C - 35°C (Requires 180-200 frost-free days and abundant sunshine)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 50% - 80%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 500 mm - 1000 mm (Low to medium rainfall; dry weather during boll ripening and harvest)
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.0 - 8.0 (Tolerant to mild alkalinity)
- **Soil Type**: Deep Black Cotton Soil (Vertisols), Alluvial soil, Heavy Loam with high moisture retention.
- **NPK Requirements**: High Nitrogen (N: 90-120 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 45-60 kg/ha), High K (K: 45-60 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 120:60:60 for hybrid varieties. Split N applications at vegetative, square formation, and boll development stages.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Kharif (May to November). Harvested in multiple pickings as bolls open.
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Gujarat, Maharashtra, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Critical irrigation at squaring, flowering, and boll development. Drip irrigation highly effective.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Bt Cotton hybrids widely grown; spacing 90x60 cm; monitor for Pink Bollworm, Whitefly, and Aphids.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Cotton (Gossypium hirsutum / arboreum) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (High Nitrogen (N: 90-120 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 45-60 kg/ha), High K (K: 45-60 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Kharif (May to November). Harvested in multiple pickings as bolls open.).
