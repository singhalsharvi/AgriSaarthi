# Orange / Mandarin (Citrus reticulata / sinensis)

- **Crop Name**: Orange / Mandarin (Citrus reticulata / sinensis)
- **Crop Category**: Sub-tropical Citrus Fruit Crop
- **Suitable Temperature**: 15°C - 35°C (Sub-tropical climate, dry bloom phase)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 50% - 75%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 900 mm - 1500 mm
- **Soil pH Range**: 5.5 - 7.5
- **Soil Type**: Deep Well-drained Loam, Sandy Loam, Clay Loam; intolerant to waterlogging and high salt.
- **NPK Requirements**: High N & K (N: 600g, P: 200g, K: 300g per tree/year)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: Apply NPK 600:200:300g per tree/year in 3 split doses (March, June, October) along with Micronutrients (Zinc, Iron, Boron).
- **Suitable Seasons**: Perennial (Nagpur Mandarin: Ambe bahar harvested Nov-Dec; Mrig bahar harvested March-April).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Maharashtra (Nagpur), Punjab, Rajasthan, Assam, Madhya Pradesh, Andhra Pradesh, Meghalaya.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Drip irrigation (20-40 liters/tree/day); stress management during bahar induction.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: T-budded plants on Rough Lemon or Rangpur Lime rootstock; spacing 6m x 6m; regular removal of water sprouts and deadwood.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Orange / Mandarin (Citrus reticulata / sinensis) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (High N & K (N: 600g, P: 200g, K: 300g per tree/year)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Perennial (Nagpur Mandarin: Ambe bahar harvested Nov-Dec; Mrig bahar harvested March-April).).
