# Sunflower (Helianthus annuus)

- **Crop Name**: Sunflower (Helianthus annuus)
- **Crop Category**: Oilseed Crop
- **Suitable Temperature**: 20°C - 32°C
- **Humidity Requirement**: 50% - 75%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 500 mm - 800 mm
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.5 - 8.0
- **Soil Type**: Deep Black soil, Loam, Sandy Loam with good drainage
- **NPK Requirements**: High N, P, K (N: 60-80 kg/ha, P: 60 kg/ha, K: 40 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 60:60:40 + 20 kg Sulphur/ha. Apply 50% N + full P & K basal.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Kharif, Rabi, and Summer.
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Punjab, Haryana.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Critical irrigation at buttoning, flowering, and seed development stages.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Seed rate 5-6 kg/ha; hand pollination / beehive placement increases seed set.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Sunflower (Helianthus annuus) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (High N, P, K (N: 60-80 kg/ha, P: 60 kg/ha, K: 40 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Kharif, Rabi, and Summer.).
