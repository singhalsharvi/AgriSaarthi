# Grapes (Vitis vinifera)

- **Crop Name**: Grapes (Vitis vinifera)
- **Crop Category**: Perennial Fruit / Vine Crop
- **Suitable Temperature**: 15°C - 35°C (Warm dry summer, cool winter)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 40% - 60% (Low humidity prevents downy mildew)
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 500 mm - 900 mm (Dry weather during flowering and berry growth)
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.5 - 8.0
- **Soil Type**: Sandy Loam, Red Loam, Light Black soil with good drainage.
- **NPK Requirements**: High Nitrogen & Potassium (N: 300-500 kg/ha, P: 200-300 kg/ha, K: 400-600 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK fertigation via drip based on pruning schedule (Foundation pruning in April, Forward pruning in Oct).
- **Suitable Seasons**: Perennial (Harvesting Feb-April in North India / Jan-April in Peninsular India).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Maharashtra (Nashik, Sangli), Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Punjab.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Drip irrigation system mandatory; withhold water before pruning and before harvest for sugar accumulation.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Bower / Trellis system of training; double pruning annual cycle; GA3 (Gibberellic acid) application for berry enlargement.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Grapes (Vitis vinifera) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (High Nitrogen & Potassium (N: 300-500 kg/ha, P: 200-300 kg/ha, K: 400-600 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Perennial (Harvesting Feb-April in North India / Jan-April in Peninsular India).).
