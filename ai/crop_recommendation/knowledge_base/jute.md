# Jute (Corchorus capsularis / olitorius)

- **Crop Name**: Jute (Corchorus capsularis / olitorius)
- **Crop Category**: Fiber Crop (Golden Fiber)
- **Suitable Temperature**: 24°C - 38°C (Warm and humid climate)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 70% - 90%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 1200 mm - 2000 mm (High rainfall during growth)
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.0 - 7.5
- **Soil Type**: New Alluvial, Loamy soil, River basin silt; fertile well-drained soils.
- **NPK Requirements**: Moderate Nitrogen (N: 40-60 kg/ha), Low-Moderate P (P: 20-30 kg/ha), Moderate K (K: 30-40 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: Basal application of FYM 5 t/ha, NPK 60:30:30. Top dress N in two splits at 3 and 6 weeks after sowing.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Pre-Kharif / Summer (March to July). Harvesting at 120-135 days (small pod stage).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: West Bengal, Assam, Bihar, Odisha, Meghalaya, Tripura.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Requires initial moist soil for germination; rainfed in high rainfall regions; avoid prolonged standing water.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Line sowing with seed rate 5-7 kg/ha; thinning at 3 weeks; Retting process in clean flowing water (14-21 days) for fiber extraction.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Jute (Corchorus capsularis / olitorius) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. Harvesting at 120-135 days (small pod stage).).
