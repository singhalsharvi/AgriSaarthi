# Watermelon (Citrullus lanatus)

- **Crop Name**: Watermelon (Citrullus lanatus)
- **Crop Category**: Cucurbit / Summer Fruit Crop
- **Suitable Temperature**: 24°C - 35°C (Warm dry climate with long sunny days)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 50% - 70%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 400 mm - 700 mm (Sensitive to high rainfall and humidity)
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.0 - 7.0
- **Soil Type**: Sandy Loam, Riverbed Alluvial soil, Well-drained rich sandy soils.
- **NPK Requirements**: Moderate N, High P & K (N: 80-100 kg/ha, P: 50-60 kg/ha, K: 80-100 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 100:50:80. Apply 50% N + full P & K at sowing, remaining N in two splits during vine growth and fruit initiation.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Summer crop (Sown Dec-Feb in plains; harvested March-May).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Uttar Pradesh, Karnataka, Punjab, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, Rajasthan.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Irrigate every 7-10 days; avoid water contact with ripening fruit; reduce irrigation near harvest for sugar build-up.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Seed rate 3-4 kg/ha; riverbed cultivation (Diara land farming) popular; mulching with silver-black polythene film.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Watermelon (Citrullus lanatus) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (Moderate N, High P & K (N: 80-100 kg/ha, P: 50-60 kg/ha, K: 80-100 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Summer crop (Sown Dec-Feb in plains; harvested March-May).).
