# Papaya (Carica papaya)

- **Crop Name**: Papaya (Carica papaya)
- **Crop Category**: Tropical Fruit Crop
- **Suitable Temperature**: 21°C - 33°C (Sensitive to frost and cold winds)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 60% - 85%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 1000 mm - 1500 mm
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.0 - 7.0
- **Soil Type**: Rich Sandy Loam, Alluvial soil with high organic content and excellent drainage.
- **NPK Requirements**: High N & K (N: 250g, P: 250g, K: 500g per plant/year)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 250:250:500g per plant per year applied in 6 bi-monthly split doses starting from 2nd month after planting.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Planted in Feb-March, June-July, or Oct-Nov. Fruits ready in 9-10 months; crop life 2-3 years.
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, West Bengal, Tamil Nadu.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Regular irrigation every 7-10 days in winter, 4-5 days in summer; strict protection against water stagnation (causes collar rot).
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Gynodioecious varieties (Red Lady, Pusa Delicious); pit size 60x60x60 cm; spacing 1.8x1.8m or 2x2m; ring weeding.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Papaya (Carica papaya) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (High N & K (N: 250g, P: 250g, K: 500g per plant/year)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Planted in Feb-March, June-July, or Oct-Nov. Fruits ready in 9-10 months; crop life 2-3 years.).
