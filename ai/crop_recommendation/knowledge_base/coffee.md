# Coffee (Coffea arabica / canephora)

- **Crop Name**: Coffee (Coffea arabica / canephora)
- **Crop Category**: Perennial Plantation Beverage Crop
- **Suitable Temperature**: 15°C - 28°C for Arabica; 20°C - 32°C for Robusta (Shade-grown tropical climate)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 70% - 90%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 1500 mm - 2500 mm (Dry phase Dec-March essential for flowering after blossom shower)
- **Soil pH Range**: 5.5 - 6.5 (Slightly acidic forest soils)
- **Soil Type**: Deep, well-drained organic rich Red/Lateritic Loam under multi-tier forest shade canopy.
- **NPK Requirements**: High N & K (N: 140-160 kg/ha, P: 105-120 kg/ha, K: 140-160 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 140:105:140 in 3-4 split applications (Pre-blossom, Post-blossom, Pre-monsoon, Post-monsoon).
- **Suitable Seasons**: Perennial (Harvesting Nov-Dec for Arabica; Jan-March for Robusta).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Karnataka (Chikmagalur, Coorg, Hassan), Kerala (Wayanad), Tamil Nadu (Nilgiris), Andhra Pradesh (Araku Valley).
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Blossom and backing showers via sprinkler irrigation in March/April if natural rainfall delayed.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Shade tree canopy management (Dadap, Silver Oak); centering and pruning post-harvest; picking ripe red cherries.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Coffee (Coffea arabica / canephora) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (High N & K (N: 140-160 kg/ha, P: 105-120 kg/ha, K: 140-160 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Perennial (Harvesting Nov-Dec for Arabica; Jan-March for Robusta).).
