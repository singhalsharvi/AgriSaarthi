# Potato (Solanum tuberosum)

- **Crop Name**: Potato (Solanum tuberosum)
- **Crop Category**: Rabi Tuber / Vegetable
- **Suitable Temperature**: 15°C - 24°C (Night temp ~15°C optimum for tuberization)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 60% - 80%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 500 mm - 750 mm water requirement
- **Soil pH Range**: 5.2 - 6.5 (Slightly acidic soil prevents potato scab)
- **Soil Type**: Loose Alluvial, Sandy Loam rich in organic matter with excellent drainage
- **NPK Requirements**: Very High Nitrogen & Potassium (N: 150-180 kg/ha, P: 80-100 kg/ha, K: 100-120 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 150:80:100 + FYM 25 t/ha. Apply 50% N + full P & K at planting, remaining N at earthing up.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Rabi (October/November planting; harvested Jan-March).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Bihar, Punjab, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Light frequent irrigation every 7-10 days; avoid wetting ridges near harvest.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Seed tuber rate 25-30 q/ha; earthing up at 30 and 45 DAS; haulm cutting 10-15 days before harvest.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Potato (Solanum tuberosum) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (Very High Nitrogen & Potassium (N: 150-180 kg/ha, P: 80-100 kg/ha, K: 100-120 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Rabi (October/November planting; harvested Jan-March).).
