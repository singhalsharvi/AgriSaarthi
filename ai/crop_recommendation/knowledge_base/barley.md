# Barley (Hordeum vulgare)

- **Crop Name**: Barley (Hordeum vulgare)
- **Crop Category**: Rabi Cereal Grain
- **Suitable Temperature**: 12°C - 24°C
- **Humidity Requirement**: 45% - 65%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 400 mm - 650 mm
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.5 - 8.5 (Tolerant to mild salinity/alkalinity)
- **Soil Type**: Sandy Loam, Light Alluvial, Loam with good drainage
- **NPK Requirements**: Moderate N (N: 60 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 30 kg/ha), Low K (K: 20 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 60:30:20 basal application.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Rabi (October-November sowing; harvested March-April).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, Punjab, Madhya Pradesh.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: 2-3 irrigations (CRI, tillering, and heading stage).
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Seed rate 80-100 kg/ha; tolerant to drought and saline soil conditions compared to wheat.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Barley (Hordeum vulgare) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (Moderate N (N: 60 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 30 kg/ha), Low K (K: 20 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Rabi (October-November sowing; harvested March-April).).
