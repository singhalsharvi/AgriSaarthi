# Black Gram / Urad (Vigna mungo)

- **Crop Name**: Black Gram / Urad (Vigna mungo)
- **Crop Category**: Short-duration Pulse Legume
- **Suitable Temperature**: 25°C - 35°C (Warm and humid climate)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 60% - 80%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 600 mm - 1000 mm
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.5 - 7.8
- **Soil Type**: Heavy Black Cotton soils, Alluvial soil, Clay Loam with good water retention.
- **NPK Requirements**: Low Nitrogen (N: 20 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 40-50 kg/ha), Low K (K: 20 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 20:40:20 kg/ha + 20 kg Sulphur/ha as basal dose. Rhizobium bio-fertilizer seed inoculation.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Kharif, Rabi (South India coastal rice fallows), and Summer.
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, Karnataka.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Rainfed in Kharif; Summer/Rabi crops require 2-3 irrigations at flowering and pod formation.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Seed rate 15-20 kg/ha; sown as pure crop or intercropped with cotton/sugarcane.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Black Gram / Urad (Vigna mungo) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (Low Nitrogen (N: 20 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 40-50 kg/ha), Low K (K: 20 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Kharif, Rabi (South India coastal rice fallows), and Summer.).
