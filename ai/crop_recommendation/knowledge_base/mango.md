# Mango (Mangifera indica)

- **Crop Name**: Mango (Mangifera indica)
- **Crop Category**: Perennial Tropical Fruit Crop (King of Fruits)
- **Suitable Temperature**: 24°C - 36°C (Requires warm dry weather during flowering/fruiting)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 50% - 75%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 750 mm - 2500 mm (Dry period essential during flowering)
- **Soil pH Range**: 5.5 - 7.5
- **Soil Type**: Deep Alluvial, Loamy, Red Lateritic well-drained soils (minimum 1.5m soil depth).
- **NPK Requirements**: High N, P, K for mature trees (N: 1000g, P: 500g, K: 1000g per tree/year)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: Apply NPK 1000:500:1000g + 50 kg FYM per tree per year for trees >10 years old in two split doses (after harvest & post-monsoon).
- **Suitable Seasons**: Perennial (Harvesting March to July depending on region and variety).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Uttar Pradesh, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Bihar, Gujarat, Maharashtra, West Bengal, Tamil Nadu.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Young trees require regular watering; bearing trees require irrigation during fruit development; withhold water 2-3 months before flowering.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Grafted varieties (Alphonso, Dasheri, Langra, Totapuri, Banganapalle); spacing 10m x 10m (traditional) or 5m x 5m (high density).

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Mango (Mangifera indica) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (High N, P, K for mature trees (N: 1000g, P: 500g, K: 1000g per tree/year)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Perennial (Harvesting March to July depending on region and variety).).
