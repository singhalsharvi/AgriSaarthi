# Lentil / Masoor (Lens culinaris)

- **Crop Name**: Lentil / Masoor (Lens culinaris)
- **Crop Category**: Rabi Pulse Legume
- **Suitable Temperature**: 15°C - 25°C (Cool climate during growth)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 45% - 65%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 450 mm - 750 mm
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.0 - 7.5
- **Soil Type**: Loam, Clay Loam, Alluvial soil, light black soil; intolerant to waterlogging.
- **NPK Requirements**: Low Nitrogen (N: 20 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 40 kg/ha), Low K (K: 20 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: 20 kg N + 40 kg P2O5 per ha basal. Seed inoculation with Rhizobium leguminosarum.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Rabi (October/November to March/April).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Rajasthan, Chhattisgarh.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: 1-2 light irrigations. First irrigation at 30-35 days (branching), second at pod formation.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Seed rate 30-40 kg/ha; spacing 30 cm; weed control with Pendimethalin pre-emergence.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Lentil / Masoor (Lens culinaris) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (Low Nitrogen (N: 20 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 40 kg/ha), Low K (K: 20 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Rabi (October/November to March/April).).
