# Chickpea / Bengal Gram (Cicer arietinum)

- **Crop Name**: Chickpea / Bengal Gram (Cicer arietinum)
- **Crop Category**: Pulse / Legume
- **Suitable Temperature**: 15°C - 28°C (Cool climate during growth, warm dry at harvest)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 40% - 65%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 600 mm - 900 mm (Drought-tolerant, grown mostly on residual soil moisture)
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.0 - 8.0 (Prefers neutral to slightly alkaline soil)
- **Soil Type**: Well-drained Black soils, Heavy Loams, Clay Loams; avoids waterlogging.
- **NPK Requirements**: Low Nitrogen (N: 20 kg/ha - nitrogen fixing), Moderate P (P: 40-50 kg/ha), Low-Moderate K (K: 20 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: DAP 100 kg/ha at sowing (supplies 18 kg N + 46 kg P2O5). Seed treatment with Rhizobium and PSB inoculants.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Rabi (October/November to February/March).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: 1-2 light irrigations if rainfed moisture insufficient. Critical stages: Pre-flowering and Pod formation.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Seed rate 75-90 kg/ha; seed treatment with Trichoderma; nipping (topping) terminal buds at 30-45 days to promote branching.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Chickpea / Bengal Gram (Cicer arietinum) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (Low Nitrogen (N: 20 kg/ha - nitrogen fixing), Moderate P (P: 40-50 kg/ha), Low-Moderate K (K: 20 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Rabi (October/November to February/March).).
