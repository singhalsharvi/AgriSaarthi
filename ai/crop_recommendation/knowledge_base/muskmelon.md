# Muskmelon / Cantaloupe (Cucumis melo)

- **Crop Name**: Muskmelon / Cantaloupe (Cucumis melo)
- **Crop Category**: Cucurbit / Summer Fruit Crop
- **Suitable Temperature**: 25°C - 35°C (Hot dry weather required for sweetness)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 45% - 65%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 400 mm - 600 mm
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.0 - 7.5
- **Soil Type**: Sandy Loam, Well-drained Alluvial riverbed soil.
- **NPK Requirements**: Moderate N & K, High P (N: 80 kg/ha, P: 50 kg/ha, K: 60 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 80:50:60. Basal application of FYM 15 t/ha + half N + full P & K. Top dress remaining N at vine development.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Summer (Sown Jan-March; harvested April-June).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Andhra Pradesh.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Light irrigation every 5-7 days; stop irrigation 5 days before harvest to enhance fruit sweetness and net formation.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Seed rate 1.5-2.0 kg/ha; channel/ridge layout; harvest at full slip stage for distant markets.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Muskmelon / Cantaloupe (Cucumis melo) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (Moderate N & K, High P (N: 80 kg/ha, P: 50 kg/ha, K: 60 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Summer (Sown Jan-March; harvested April-June).).
