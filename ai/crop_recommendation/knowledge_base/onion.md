# Onion (Allium cepa)

- **Crop Name**: Onion (Allium cepa)
- **Crop Category**: Bulb Vegetable Crop
- **Suitable Temperature**: 15°C - 30°C (Cool vegetative growth, warm dry bulb ripening)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 50% - 70%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 500 mm - 800 mm
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.0 - 7.5
- **Soil Type**: Light Loam, Sandy Loam, Red Loam rich in humus with good drainage
- **NPK Requirements**: High N & K (N: 100 kg/ha, P: 50 kg/ha, K: 50 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 100:50:50 + 30 kg Sulphur/ha (essential for pungent bulb flavor). Apply N in 2 splits.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Rabi (Main), Kharif, and Late Kharif.
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Maharashtra (Nashik), Karnataka, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Andhra Pradesh.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Light irrigations every 7-10 days; stop irrigation 15 days before harvest.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Nursery transplanting 7-8 week old seedlings; spacing 15x10 cm; neck fall is harvest indicator.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Onion (Allium cepa) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (High N & K (N: 100 kg/ha, P: 50 kg/ha, K: 50 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Rabi (Main), Kharif, and Late Kharif.).
