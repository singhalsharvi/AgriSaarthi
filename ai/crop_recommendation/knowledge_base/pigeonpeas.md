# Pigeonpea / Arhar / Tur (Cajanus cajan)

- **Crop Name**: Pigeonpea / Arhar / Tur (Cajanus cajan)
- **Crop Category**: Pulse / Legume
- **Suitable Temperature**: 20°C - 35°C (Warm tropical climate)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 50% - 75%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 600 mm - 1000 mm (Deep rooted, highly drought resistant)
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.5 - 7.5
- **Soil Type**: Deep Alluvial, Black Cotton soils, Loam with good internal drainage.
- **NPK Requirements**: Low Nitrogen (N: 20 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 50 kg/ha), Low K (K: 20 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: Basal application of 100 kg DAP/ha + 20 kg Sulphur/ha. Inoculate seeds with Rhizobium culture.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Kharif (June/July sowing; harvested Nov-Jan for medium/long duration varieties).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, Gujarat, Telangana.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Mostly rainfed. If dry spell occurs, 1-2 irrigations at flower initiation and pod filling stage.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Often intercropped with sorghum, maize, or soybean (1:2 or 1:4 ratio); seed rate 12-15 kg/ha.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Pigeonpea / Arhar / Tur (Cajanus cajan) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (Low Nitrogen (N: 20 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 50 kg/ha), Low K (K: 20 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Kharif (June/July sowing; harvested Nov-Jan for medium/long duration varieties).).
