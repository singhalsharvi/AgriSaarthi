# Tea (Camellia sinensis)

- **Crop Name**: Tea (Camellia sinensis)
- **Crop Category**: Perennial Plantation Beverage Crop
- **Suitable Temperature**: 13°C - 30°C (Highland sub-tropical/tropical climate)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 75% - 95%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 1500 mm - 3000 mm well-distributed
- **Soil pH Range**: 4.5 - 5.5 (Acidic soil requirement)
- **Soil Type**: Deep, well-drained acidic Red/Lateritic Loam rich in organic matter on hill slopes
- **NPK Requirements**: High N & K (N: 150 kg/ha, P: 40 kg/ha, K: 120 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 150:40:120 applied in 3-4 split doses during plucking season.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Perennial (Plucking throughout warm months).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Assam, West Bengal (Darjeeling), Tamil Nadu (Nilgiris), Kerala (Munnar), Himachal Pradesh (Kangra).
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Mostly rainfed; sprinkler irrigation used during dry period (Jan-April).
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Shade tree planting (Albizia); contour planting on slopes; plucking two leaves and a bud every 7-10 days.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Tea (Camellia sinensis) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (High N & K (N: 150 kg/ha, P: 40 kg/ha, K: 120 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Perennial (Plucking throughout warm months).).
