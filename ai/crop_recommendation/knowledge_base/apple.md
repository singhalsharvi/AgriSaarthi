# Apple (Malus domestica)

- **Crop Name**: Apple (Malus domestica)
- **Crop Category**: Temperate Fruit Plantation Crop
- **Suitable Temperature**: 7°C - 24°C (Requires 1000-1500 chilling hours below 7°C)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 60% - 75%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 1000 mm - 1250 mm well-distributed during growth
- **Soil pH Range**: 5.5 - 6.5 (Slightly acidic loam)
- **Soil Type**: Deep, well-drained Loamy soils rich in organic matter (minimum 1.5m depth).
- **NPK Requirements**: High N & K for bearing trees (N: 700g, P: 350g, K: 700g per mature tree/year)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 700:350:700g per tree per year applied in winter (Jan-Feb) along with 50 kg well-rotted FYM.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Perennial (Harvesting July to October depending on altitude and variety).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Arunachal Pradesh.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Critical irrigation during fruit set and fruit development (May to August).
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Grafted on seedling or clonal rootstocks (M9, MM106); spacing 4m x 4m or high density 2m x 1m; regular winter pruning and fruit thinning.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Apple (Malus domestica) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (High N & K for bearing trees (N: 700g, P: 350g, K: 700g per mature tree/year)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Perennial (Harvesting July to October depending on altitude and variety).).
