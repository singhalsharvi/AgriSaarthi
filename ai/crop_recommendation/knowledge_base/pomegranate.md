# Pomegranate (Punica granatum)

- **Crop Name**: Pomegranate (Punica granatum)
- **Crop Category**: Arid Fruit Plantation Crop
- **Suitable Temperature**: 20°C - 38°C (Hot dry summer and cold winter)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 35% - 60% (Prefers dry atmosphere during fruit development)
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 500 mm - 800 mm
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.5 - 8.0 (Tolerant to mild salinity and alkalinity)
- **Soil Type**: Deep Loamy, Sandy Loam, Light Black soil with good drainage.
- **NPK Requirements**: High N & K (N: 600g, P: 250g, K: 600g per mature plant/year)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: Apply 625g N, 250g P2O5, 625g K2O per tree per year along with 20-30 kg FYM during Bahar regulation.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Perennial (Bahar regulation: Ambe Bahar, Hasta Bahar, or Mrig Bahar).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Maharashtra, Gujarat, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Rajasthan, Telangana.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Drip irrigation highly recommended (20-30 liters/tree/day depending on season).
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Propagated through stem cuttings/air layering; spacing 4.5m x 4.5m or 5m x 5m; prune water shoots and regulate crop load.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Pomegranate (Punica granatum) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (High N & K (N: 600g, P: 250g, K: 600g per mature plant/year)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Perennial (Bahar regulation: Ambe Bahar, Hasta Bahar, or Mrig Bahar).).
