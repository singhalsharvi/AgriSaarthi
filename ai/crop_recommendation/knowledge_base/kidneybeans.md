# Kidney Beans / Rajma (Phaseolus vulgaris)

- **Crop Name**: Kidney Beans / Rajma (Phaseolus vulgaris)
- **Crop Category**: Pulse / Grain Legume
- **Suitable Temperature**: 15°C - 25°C (Cool sub-tropical climate)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 50% - 70%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 600 mm - 1200 mm
- **Soil pH Range**: 5.5 - 6.5 (Slightly acidic to neutral)
- **Soil Type**: Light Loam, Silt Loam, Deep fertile well-drained soils.
- **NPK Requirements**: High Nitrogen compared to other pulses (N: 80-100 kg/ha - inefficient N fixation), Moderate P (P: 50-60 kg/ha), Moderate K (K: 40-50 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 100:60:40. Apply 50% N + full P & K at sowing, remaining 50% N at first weeding (30 DAS).
- **Suitable Seasons**: Rabi in plains (Oct-Nov); Kharif in hilly northern regions (May-June).
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, Uttarakhand, Bihar, Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: 3-4 irrigations required. Sensitive to both drought and waterlogging. Critical stages: Flowering and Pod filling.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Seed rate 80-100 kg/ha; line spacing 30-45 cm; weed management at 20-25 days.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Kidney Beans / Rajma (Phaseolus vulgaris) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (High Nitrogen compared to other pulses (N: 80-100 kg/ha - inefficient N fixation), Moderate P (P: 50-60 kg/ha), Moderate K (K: 40-50 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Rabi in plains (Oct-Nov); Kharif in hilly northern regions (May-June).).
