# Jowar / Sorghum (Sorghum bicolor)

- **Crop Name**: Jowar / Sorghum (Sorghum bicolor)
- **Crop Category**: Coarse Cereal / Dryland Grain
- **Suitable Temperature**: 26°C - 35°C
- **Humidity Requirement**: 45% - 70%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 400 mm - 750 mm (Drought-tolerant)
- **Soil pH Range**: 6.0 - 8.5
- **Soil Type**: Deep Black Cotton soil, Clay Loam, Medium Black soil
- **NPK Requirements**: Moderate N (N: 80 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 40 kg/ha), Moderate K (K: 40 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 80:40:40 kg/ha. Apply half N + full P & K at sowing.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Kharif and Rabi.
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Maharashtra, Karnataka, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: Rainfed in Kharif; 2-3 irrigations for Rabi crop.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Seed rate 8-10 kg/ha; spacing 45x15 cm; shoot fly management at early seedling stage.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Jowar / Sorghum (Sorghum bicolor) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (Moderate N (N: 80 kg/ha), Moderate P (P: 40 kg/ha), Moderate K (K: 40 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Kharif and Rabi.).
