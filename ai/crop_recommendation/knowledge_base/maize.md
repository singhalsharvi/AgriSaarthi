# Maize / Corn (Zea mays)

- **Crop Name**: Maize / Corn (Zea mays)
- **Crop Category**: Cereal / Coarse Grain
- **Suitable Temperature**: 18°C - 32°C (Optimum ~21°C - 27°C)
- **Humidity Requirement**: 55% - 75%
- **Rainfall Requirement**: 500 mm - 1000 mm (Well-distributed rainfall)
- **Soil pH Range**: 5.8 - 7.5 (Well-drained neutral soil)
- **Soil Type**: Deep Loam, Silt Loam, Alluvial soil with excellent drainage; sensitive to waterlogging.
- **NPK Requirements**: High Nitrogen (N: 100-120 kg/ha), Moderate Phosphorus (P: 50-60 kg/ha), Moderate Potassium (K: 40-50 kg/ha)
- **Fertilizer Guidance**: NPK 120:60:50. Apply 1/3 N + full P & K at sowing, remaining N split at knee-high and tasseling stages.
- **Suitable Seasons**: Kharif (Primary), Rabi (Bihar, AP, Tamil Nadu), and Spring/Summer.
- **Top Indian Producing Regions**: Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh.
- **Water & Irrigation Requirements**: 4-6 irrigations depending on rainfall. Critical stages: Tasseling, Silking, and Grain filling.
- **Cultivation & Agronomic Practices**: Ridge and furrow planting; seed rate 20 kg/ha; weed control using Atrazine pre-emergence; monitor for Fall Armyworm.

## Detailed Agronomic Overview
Maize / Corn (Zea mays) thrives best when grown under its optimal environmental and soil conditions. It requires balanced NPK fertilization (High Nitrogen (N: 100-120 kg/ha), Moderate Phosphorus (P: 50-60 kg/ha), Moderate Potassium (K: 40-50 kg/ha)), appropriate irrigation scheduling, and proper field management tailored to Indian agricultural seasons (Kharif (Primary), Rabi (Bihar, AP, Tamil Nadu), and Spring/Summer.).
